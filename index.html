The range

Member amenities

Unlimited range time

Shoot as often as you like for $3 a session. Targets are free.

Cleaning area

Cleaning supplies and a work area for members. No live ammunition in the cleaning area.

Locker rental

Store eye and ear protection, range gear and cleaning supplies. No firearms or ammunition in lockers.

Refreshments & notary

Coffee, tea and soda for a small fee. Notary services for members only.

Handguns only.

Stuyvesant is a pistol range. No rifles, no shotguns and no magnum calibers on the line. Obey the range officer or instructor on duty at all times.

Wash your hands with soap and cold water after shooting.

Range rules

  1. Treat every firearm as if it is loaded.
  2. Always keep the firearm pointed in a safe direction.
  3. Keep your finger off the trigger until ready to shoot.
  4. Keep the firearm unloaded until ready to use.
  5. Know how to safely operate your firearm.
  6. Use only the correct ammunition for your firearm.
  7. Wear appropriate eye and ear protection.
  8. Wash hands thoroughly with soap and cold water after shooting.
  9. No alcohol, marijuana or any substance, legal or illegal, that may impair safe handling.
  10. Keep the muzzle downrange at all times, even when clearing a malfunction.
  11. No disruptive behavior or distractions while others are shooting.
  12. Show proper etiquette and respect for other members.
  13. Carry the appropriate licenses and documentation.
  14. No live ammunition in the cleaning area.
  15. No firearms or ammunition stored in personal lockers.
  16. Push brass out to the range floor, remove used targets and clear trash from ports.
  17. Handguns only. No rifles, shotguns or magnum calibers.
  18. Obey all commands of the range officer or instructor on duty.
  19. Above all, safety is the top priority.
